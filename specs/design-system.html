Wayrest

A design system for the journey, not just the booking.

Wayrest is a place to find rest along the way — a waypoint on a longer route. The system is warm, grounded, and tactile: the glow of a lantern at the trailhead, the hush of an inn at dusk.

Spacing & Layout

03 — Rhythm

A 4 px base grid. Use multiples of 4 for everything — padding, gap, margin. Larger steps skip ahead so layouts feel composed, not arithmetic.

Border Radius

04 — Curvature

Soft but not bubbly. Inputs and buttons share md. Cards step up to lg. Hero imagery and modals use xl. Pills and avatars use full.

Elevation

05 — Shadows

Shadows are tinted with warm brown (#40392E), never neutral gray. They sit lightly — the goal is depth, not drama.
